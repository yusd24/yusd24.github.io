At various points in your academic journey, you may need a letter of recommendation from a professor. 
Throughout my own education and career, my mentors spent a significant amount of time and energy writing recommendation 
letters for me, so I take this responsibility very seriously. 

I am more than happy to help write letters for students applying 
to various positions, including graduate programs, transfers, summer research projects, and more.

If we have had one-on-one interactions (such as during office hours or on individual/group projects) and the programs you are applying 
to are closely related to mathematics, my letter may be beneficial to your application.

If you would like to request a recommendation letter, please follow the guidelines below.

---

#### Initial request

Please email me at least four weeks before your first deadline. Your email must include:

- Confirmation that you have read this page.
- A brief reminder of who you are and how we know each other, especially if it has been a while since we last interacted.
- A list of the programs you are applying to, along with their respective deadlines.
- Specific instructions on how the recommendation letters should be submitted.

  
---

#### Supplementary materials

Once I reply agreeing to write the letter, I may ask you to provide additional materials, such as:

- Drafts of your application materials (e.g., your research statement).

Additionally, depending on the nature of your application, I may schedule an online or in-person meeting with you if necessary.


---
  
  
#### Reminders and follow-up

Feel free to send me email reminders as the deadline approaches—you won't be bothering me! 
I will also notify you via email once I have submitted your letter. Finally, please be sure to let me know the outcome 
of your applications. Even if the results are not what you hoped for, please keep me updated; 
I may still be able to offer some follow-up advice.
